# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
* Demonstrating empathy and professional courtesy toward other contributors.
* Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and engineering feedback.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical reviews.
* Focusing on what is best for the overall reliability and maintainability of the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at `abdallahdiouf.dev@gmail.com`.
